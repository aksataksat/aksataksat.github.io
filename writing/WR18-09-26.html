Aksat Upadhyay.

Document on Document

Writing is one of those things that I believe one should do even if they are terrible at it (me, for an example) for it is one of the few mediums where we can express our thoughts without any fear of being judged, if you are perturbed by someone in your life and find yourself in a position where you cannot speak up to them, you can always write your feelings down, the paper will never object to your feelings, (unless of course, the wrong person gets hold of the paper, that might be trouble, but you can always just burn the paper down can’t you?). 

Is this decadent age, where most mediums of expression are controlled and monitored by larger authorities, where privacy is a premium commodity and where most leisure activities actively harm your mind, writing is  an art of the bygone yesteryears where none of this was true. You can keep your writing private, you do not need to write for the sake of impressing anyone, you can write whatsoever you want, be as creative as you want; you can come up with magical worlds and get lost in them, you can write letters to your future self, or you can write about why writing is so awesome, oh wait.
